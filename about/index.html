About Reincarnation

Why Reincarnation

A technology-enabled customer experience and business operations partner.

Reincarnation was founded by leaders with experience in global outsourcing. The business supports organisations through customer experience operations, BPO consulting, workforce development and technology solutions.

Our approach is practical: understand the operating need, define responsibilities clearly and combine people, process and technology in a way the client can sustain.

01

Customer care operations

Inbound and outbound service, telesales, collections, retention, product support and technical support.

02

BPO consulting

Operational setup, workflow improvement, analytics, dashboarding and workforce-management planning.

03

Training & workforce development

Programs designed to strengthen service capability, support internal growth and help close practical talent gaps.

04

Technology solutions

CRM, digital channels, productivity tools, monitoring, automation and other solutions that support modern operations.

How we deliver

Solutions shaped around the operation.

  • Start with the workflow.Understand customers, teams, systems and service expectations before defining the solution.
  • Connect the channels.Plan customer care and CRM workflows across the communication tools the operation actually uses.
  • Make performance visible.Use clear reporting, dashboards and ownership so teams can review and improve the work.
