# Reincarnation content audit

Audit date: 28 October 2026

## Editorial standard

- Use the Reincarnation name consistently.
- Describe services in specific, plain language.
- Do not inherit another company's clients, awards, team, location, history or performance results.
- Distinguish professional services from regulated legal, tax, accounting and investment advice.
- Treat prices as planning guidance until a written scope and estimate exist.
- Publish case studies only after ownership, client permission and results are verified.

## Required before production

1. Supply the legal operating entity, registered address and company number.
2. Supply a real public email address, telephone number and social profiles if they should appear.
3. Implement and test the `/api/contact` endpoint, including spam protection, validation and retention rules.
4. Have the legal and privacy pages reviewed for the jurisdictions where the service operates.
5. Add only case studies, testimonials, client logos and metrics that can be evidenced and published with permission.
6. Supply the final public domain so canonical links, Open Graph URLs and the Webflow domain metadata can be updated.
